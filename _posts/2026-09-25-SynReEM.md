## Abstract

Serial-section volume electron microscopy (vEM) enables 3D reconstruction of synapses, but its coarse axial sampling makes structures difficult to connect reliably between sections. We present SynReEM, a framework that encodes synaptic structure so that a semantic segmentation model can distinguish individual synapses. During training, biological priors impose continuity and inclusion constraints, while online pseudo-labels guide learning. A shared feature representation feeds separate semantic and instance prediction branches. Their outputs are combined and processed with watershed segmentation to recover individual synapses. Evaluations on Synapse178, AC3/AC4, and SynWTAD show improved reconstruction performance across three anisotropic vEM datasets. Code, dataset links, and reconstruction videos are available at [https://github.com/fenglingbai/SynReEM](https://github.com/fenglingbai/SynReEM).
